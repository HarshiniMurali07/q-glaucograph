The claim

To the best of my knowledge, this will be the first hybrid quantum graph neural network that learns directly from the retinal vascular graph, applied to glaucoma severity staging and validated against parameter-matched classical models, including runs on real quantum hardware.

Positioned honestly

Closest prior work: CQH-MPN, a classical–quantum hybrid prototype network for early glaucoma diagnosis (IEEE JBHI, 2025).

The claim here is limited to the combination shown: a quantum graph network learning from the retinal vessel graph, for severity staging.

Results will be reported on accuracy, parameter efficiency, robustness and communication cost, so the work stays valuable even if the quantum layer gives no accuracy gain.

Four objectives

  • Retinal graph and vascular biomarkers
  • Hybrid quantum graph learning for severity staging
  • Causal pathway with counterfactual explanations
  • Structure-to-function prediction with uncertainty, trained across hospitals
